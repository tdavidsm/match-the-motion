🚗 Match the Motion

You'll get a graph of motion — either position vs. time or velocity vs. time. Your job is to move the car so its motion matches the graph.

  • Press Start, then drag the car across the scene as the timer runs.
  • The house is 0. Right is positive, left is negative.
  • Position–time: the car's location should follow the line — including where it starts.
  • Velocity–time: the car should move at each speed for each part. Start anywhere — only the speeds matter.
  • Get about 60% or better to pass. Use Show me in warm-ups to watch the correct motion.

To win: pass two position–time and two velocity–time warm-ups, then clear 7 three-part challenges.
